# Simple Bracket — Example Prompt

## Goal
Create an L-bracket 80mm x 60mm x 10mm with two 6mm mounting holes.

## Plan
- Step 1: Sketch 80x60 rectangle on XY plane
- Step 2: Pad 10mm
- Step 3: Sketch 60x50 vertical leg rectangle on top face
- Step 4: Pad 40mm
- Step 5: Sketch two 6mm circles on top of vertical leg
- Step 6: Pocket through-all for holes

## Expected Result
L-shaped bracket with two through-holes.
