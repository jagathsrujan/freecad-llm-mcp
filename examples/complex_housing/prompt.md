# Complex Housing — Example Prompt

## Goal
Create a hollow rectangular housing 100x80x60mm with wall thickness 5mm and a top access opening.

## Plan
- Step 1: Sketch 100x80 rectangle on XY plane
- Step 2: Pad 60mm (outer shell)
- Step 3: Sketch 90x70 rectangle on top face, centered
- Step 4: Pocket 55mm (leaving 5mm bottom wall)
- Step 5: Sketch 60x40 rectangle on top face, centered
- Step 6: Pocket through 5mm for top opening

## Expected Result
Hollow housing with 5mm walls and a rectangular opening on top.
