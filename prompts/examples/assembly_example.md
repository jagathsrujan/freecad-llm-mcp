# Assembly Example — Worked Prompt

## Goal
Assemble two parts with a bolted joint.

## Parts
1. Bracket (from bracket_example)
2. Flat plate: 100mm x 60mm x 5mm with two 6mm holes at same spacing as bracket

## Execution Plan

### Step 1: Create Plate
Create flat plate with two through holes

### Step 2: Insert Parts
Insert both parts into assembly document

### Step 3: Concentric Mate 1
Align first hole on bracket with first hole on plate

### Step 4: Concentric Mate 2
Align second hole on bracket with second hole on plate

### Step 5: Coincident Mate
Make bracket bottom face flush with plate top face

## Validation
- Interference check between bracket and plate
- Verify both mates are satisfied
- Check distance between holes matches (20mm)
